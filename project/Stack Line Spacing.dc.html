A home espresso corner that actually works
Two years of trial and error, condensed. Buy in this order.
The setup
01
Start with the grinder
It matters more than the machine. A good burr grinder makes a cheap machine taste expensive.
Visit site ↗
02
Get a scale with a timer
Weigh the beans going in and the shot coming out. Guessing is where most bad shots come from.
Visit site ↗
Dialing in
03
Buy fresh, local beans
Roasted within the last three weeks. Anything older won't give you crema.
04
Aim for 1:2 in 28 seconds
18g in, 36g out. Adjust the grind, not the dose, until you hit it.
Keeping it running
05
Use filtered water
Scale builds up fast with hard tap water. A pitcher filter is enough.
Visit site ↗
06
Backflush once a week
Five minutes with a blind basket keeps the group head clean and shots consistent.
